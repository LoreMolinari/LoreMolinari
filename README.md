  ## 🎓 About Me

  I'm a **Software Engineer at adesso Schweiz AG** in Lugano, working on enterprise applications AI-assisted workflows and cybersecurity. I hold a **double Master's degree in Informatics** from **Università della Svizzera italiana** (USI, Lugano) and **Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca** (UNIMIB, Milan), and a **BSc in Computer Systems and Networks Security**from **Università degli Studi di Milano**.

  My master's thesis, *Privacy Leakage of Counterfactual Explanations in Federated Learning*, studies how explanations in federated learning can make membership-inference attacks easier, and how differential privacy trades that risk against model utility. It was recognised as one of Italy's top 100 theses by **Forbes Italia (2025)**.                          

  ### 🌟 Current Focus
  - 🔬 **Research**: Privacy-preserving machine learning, federated learning, and explainable AI
  - 🛡️ **Security**: Threat detection and security data pipelines, secure-by-design AI systems
  - 🤖 **Engineering**: AI-assisted development and automation, OutSystems and low-code platforms
  - 📚 **Learning**: Advanced cryptography, trustworthy human–AI interaction, and secure systems
